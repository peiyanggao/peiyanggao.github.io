Alzheimer’s Disease

01 · Nature Genetics · 2026-09-14
Human genetics

APOE-stratified genome-wide association analyses provide insights into the genetic etiology of Alzheimers's disease.

Thomassen JQ, Leonard H, Ulms B et al.

APOE-stratified GWAS analyses identified nine new AD risk signals that emerged when participants were grouped by ε4 or ε2 carrier status. DDHD1 showed a protective association in ε4 carriers and supporting expression-related evidence, highlighting genetic differences that pooled analyses may obscure.

02 · Science Advances · 2026-09-18
Human observations + AD mouse model

Targeting glial PD-1/PD-L1 restores microglial homeostasis and reduces neuronal hyperactivity in an Alzheimer's disease model.

Park T, Chang L, Chung SW et al.

Intracortical PD-L1 blockade in 8–10-month-old 5xFAD mice improved microglial process convergence and increased P2RY12 expression, alongside reduced neuronal hyperactivity. Astrocyte-specific PD-L1 knockdown produced similar effects, implicating local glial checkpoint signaling rather than establishing a clinical benefit of systemic immunotherapy.

Aging

03 · Neuron · 2026-09-18
Human tissues + mouse and cellular experiments

A miR-10a-5p-γCaMKII axis links periphery-to-brain signaling to cognitive vulnerability during female midlife.

Qu J, Yang J, Li W et al.

Senescence-associated features were elevated in middle-aged female brain and spleen tissues. Mouse experiments linked splenic miR-10a-5p and repression of γCaMKII to mitochondrial function, senescence, and memory; miR-10a-5p inhibition also attenuated disease-associated phenotypes in AD patient-derived neurons and model mice.

Journal article ↗ · PubMed PMID: 42759510

04 · Nature Medicine · 2026-09-16
Human multiomics + longitudinal analyses

Sex-specific biological aging clocks across organs and omics.

MULTI Consortium, Song Z, Feng D et al.

Researchers developed 38 sex-specific aging clocks across 15 organ systems. Genetic, proteomic, and longitudinal analyses revealed sex-dependent aging signatures and associations with disease and mortality, while brain-aging measures showed sex-dependent associations with cognitive decline in a preclinical AD trial. The findings support sex-aware modeling without implying that pooled models lack value.

Journal article ↗ · PubMed PMID: 42749890

05 · Science Advances · 2026-09-16
Endothelial mechanisms + mouse experiments

Endothelial C/EBPβ/AEP pathway drives vascular deterioration and systemic aging.

Li B, Nie S, Wang M et al.

Endothelial C/EBPβ–AEP activation promoted NAMPT cleavage, NAD+ depletion, and vascular deterioration in experimental models. AEP deletion or cleavage-resistant NAMPT improved vascular aging and extended lifespan in Tie2-C/EBPβ transgenic mice; AEP inhibition and NMN supplementation also alleviated vascular decline in the tested models.

Journal article ↗ · PubMed PMID: 42748238

06 · Nature Communications · 2026-09-16
Replicative cellular senescence + proteomics

Quantitative proteomics reveals coordinated changes in the proteome during replicative senescence.

Da Silva Fernandes N, Kielisch F, Fulzele A et al.

Time-resolved mass spectrometry revealed coordinated proteome remodeling during replicative senescence, including chromatin protein depletion, loss of cytoplasmic translation machinery, and increased mitochondrial protein insolubility. Reduced autophagic and proteasomal activity accompanied altered ubiquitin signaling, providing a resource for studying senescence-associated proteostasis changes.

Journal article ↗ · PubMed PMID: 42749737

07 · Science Advances · 2026-09-18
T cell epigenetics + functional experiments

Epigenetic training licenses naïve CD8+ T cell metabolic fitness and function.

Lv M, Ding J, Long H et al.

H2A.Z deposition at oxidative phosphorylation gene promoters primed naïve CD8+ T cells for activation. Age-related loss of IL-7 signaling reduced H2A.Z occupancy and impaired activation, while IL-7 supplementation or enforced H2A.Z expression rescued the defect in the experimental systems, linking immune aging to metabolic and epigenetic preparedness.
