This study included 1,078 non-demented adults from the Chinese Alzheimer's Biomarker and LifestylE (CABLE) cohort and evaluated associations of the Australian National University Alzheimer Disease Risk Index (ANU-ADRI) with cognition and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) biomarkers.

**Key findings:**

- Higher ANU-ADRI scores were associated with lower MMSE and MoCA scores.
- Higher risk scores were associated with greater CSF total tau, phosphorylated tau, and total tau/Aβ42.
- CSF total tau and phosphorylated tau partially mediated the associations between ANU-ADRI scores and cognition, with mediation proportions of 4.45% to 10.50%.
- Serial mediation through tau pathology and soluble TREM2 was not supported.

These results link a multidomain dementia risk score to tau-related pathology and cognition in non-demented adults, while remaining consistent with the observational design of the study.
