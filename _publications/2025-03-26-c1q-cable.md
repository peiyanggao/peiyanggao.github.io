This study analysed 1,264 cognitively intact participants from the Chinese Alzheimer's Biomarker and LifestylE (CABLE) cohort to investigate associations between plasma complement C1q and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease pathology.

**Key findings:**

- Plasma C1q levels differed across biomarker-defined stages of preclinical Alzheimer's disease pathology.
- Higher C1q was associated with lower CSF Aβ1-42 and Aβ1-42/Aβ1-40.
- Higher C1q was also associated with higher phosphorylated-tau/Aβ1-42 and total-tau/Aβ1-42 ratios.
- The observed pattern linked elevated peripheral C1q to a less favourable CSF amyloid and tau biomarker profile.

The findings support an association between complement activation and preclinical Alzheimer's disease biomarker changes, without establishing a causal relationship.
